## What Steel Man Does

A person with no resume and a felony conviction opens the platform on their phone.
Fifteen minutes later they have a resume tailored to a specific job, a plan for
disclosing their record in the interview, and a follow-up reminder already
scheduled.

Steel Man handles the fragmentation: narrative reconstruction, resume building,
disclosure coaching, interview practice, and fair-chance employer matching -- in
one place, free, with no login required to start.

Two surfaces:

- **The Forge** -- free, no account required, runs on any phone. Guided
  self-understanding and a first career narrative in under 20 minutes.
- **The Refinery** -- the full authenticated workshop. Tailored resumes, disclosure
  planning, voice interview practice, job matching, application tracking.

The code is licensed open-source (AGPL-3.0). Any organization can fork it, deploy
it, and build on it after August 15.
