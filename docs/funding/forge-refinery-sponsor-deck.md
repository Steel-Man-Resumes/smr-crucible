## Slide 1 -- The Promise

**Headline:** Forge and Refinery launch nationally in August.

- Free AI career infrastructure for justice-impacted people.
- Turns a person's story into employment-ready materials and a real next
  step.
- The promise: the person rebuilding their life never pays for core access.

---

## Slide 2 -- Why It Matters

**Headline:** Re-entry employment support is fragmented. This closes the gap.

- Career support for justice-impacted people is scattered across workshops,
  job boards, and case managers with no shared context.
- People need resumes, disclosure coaching, interview practice, job
  targeting, and resource navigation, in one place.
- Organizations need visibility into progress without violating a person's
  trust or privacy.

---

## Slide 3 -- What It Does

**Headline:** One platform, two entry points, built for trust.

- **The Forge:** free, no login, guided self-understanding and a first
  resume.
- **The Refinery:** the full journey, tailored resumes, disclosure planning,
  interview practice, fair-chance employer connections, and a user-directed
  AI coach.
- **Partner Dashboard:** consent-gated, so support organizations see only
  what a user chooses to share.
- Posture: coaching, not legal advice. We store progress, never transcripts.
