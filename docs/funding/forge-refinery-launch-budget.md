# Forge and Refinery -- What Launch Funding Covers

A one-page reference for sponsor conversations. Figures are ranges for
conversation purposes, not a final invoice.

## Coverage Window

90 to 180 days from the August 2026 national launch date.

## Direct Costs

| Category | What It Covers |
|---|---|
| Hosting and AI usage | Serving the Forge and Refinery nationally, including the AI coaching experience, at launch-scale traffic. |
| Participant onboarding | Guided intake, orientation, and first-session support for new users. |
| User support | Ongoing help for people using the platform during the launch window. |
| Partner onboarding | Setup and training for re-entry organizations using the consent-gated partner dashboard. |
| Accessibility and compliance review | Plain-language and accessibility passes so the platform works for the people who need it most. |
| National launch communications | Demo materials, launch messaging, and public-facing collateral. |
| Impact reporting | Aggregate, anonymized outcome tracking and a sponsor-facing impact report. |

## What Is Not in This Budget

- Individual user fees of any kind. Core access is free and stays free.
- Sale or transfer of individual user data. Reporting is aggregate and
  anonymized, consistent with our privacy posture.

## Sponsorship Tiers (Conversation Starting Points)

- **Launch supporter:** covers a defined slice of the launch window, such as
  onboarding or accessibility review.
- **Anchor sponsor:** covers the core infrastructure for the full 90 to 180
  day window and receives recognition as the national launch sponsor.
- **Cohort sponsor:** funds a named group (a city, region, employer
  partnership, or organization cohort) with its own onboarding and outcomes
  report.

Exact figures are set per conversation based on scope. Reach out for the
current numbers behind each tier.
