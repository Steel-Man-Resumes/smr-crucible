# Steel Man
## Free AI career infrastructure for justice-impacted people.

steelmanresumes.com

---

## Start here. No account required.

Open steelmanresumes.com on any phone. The Forge guides you through a 20-minute
self-understanding session. At the end you have a career narrative, a readiness
assessment, and a first resume -- ready to tailor, download, or take into the
next step.

No internet required for the basics. No login required to start.

---

## What it does

**The Forge** -- free, no account, works on any phone

- Guided career story reconstruction
- Work history, strengths, barriers, what you want next
- First resume built from your answers
- Readiness stage assessment (where you are, what helps from here)

**The Refinery** -- free, email signup

- Resume builder -- tailored to a specific job, ATS-scored
- Disclosure planner -- how and when to talk about your record
- Interview practice -- text and voice, with feedback
- Fair-chance job board -- matched to your profile, Wisconsin employers verified
- Application tracker and follow-up reminders
