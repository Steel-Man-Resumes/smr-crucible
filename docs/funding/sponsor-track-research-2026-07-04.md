# Sponsor-Track Research (Deep Research Pass)

Date: 2026-07-04
Companion to: `forge-refinery-funding-options-2026-07-04.md`
Status: research findings, no commits requested.

## What This Pass Was Looking For

Fast, direct-to-vendor sponsor cash for the August 2026 launch: money that
does not require 501(c)(3)/nonprofit status, SAM.gov registration, or a
multi-month federal grant cycle, obtainable in roughly 30-60 days. Explicitly
excluded the grant/nonprofit paths already covered in the main funding
options doc (DOL REO, BJA/Second Chance Act, Grants.gov, NBA Foundation,
Google.org, Rockefeller, Fast Forward, Claude Corps).

## Headline Finding

This is a rigorous negative result, not a lead list. Every category checked,
fair-chance employer CSR programs, bank CRA/foundation grants, and tech
"AI for good" programs, was closed to a for-profit vendor or individual
founder, too slow for a 30-60 day window, or both.

## What Was Checked and Ruled Out

- **JPMorgan Chase Second Chance Agenda / Second Chance Business Coalition**
  (50 member companies): real, well-funded internal hiring and policy
  advocacy program. No disclosed mechanism, application, or dollar figure
  for funding an external vendor or founder. The Coalition's "Become a
  Partner" page is a listing request for community organizations, not a
  funding intake.
- **Bank of America, U.S. Bank Foundation (Community Possible), Associated
  Bank**: all require 501(c)(3) status, explicitly exclude individuals
  and/or for-profits in written eligibility criteria, and run on slow,
  seasonal cycles (Associated Bank reviews February 1 to October 1 only).
- **CRA Network of Wisconsin**: not a funder. A bank-to-bank matchmaking and
  networking body for existing community-development projects (housing,
  small business, agriculture). Reentry/workforce technology is not a
  stated priority.
- **NVIDIA Inception**: non-cash perks only (training, cloud credits,
  investor intros), generic commercial AI startup program, no fit for
  public-benefit framing.
- **Checkr.org (Checkr's corporate foundation)**: mission statement exists,
  but no public eligibility criteria, application process, deadline, or
  amount. As a private foundation, funding a for-profit would require an
  atypical "expenditure responsibility" process. Checkr's own fair-chance
  ecosystem partners are established nonprofits, not vendors.
- **GitLab Foundation AI for Economic Opportunity Fund**: grants go to
  nonprofit/academic "frontline organizations" only; for-profits allowed
  only case-by-case through a nonprofit fiscal sponsor. Current cohort
  already closed; next cycle timing does not fit an August launch.
- **Stand Together Ventures Lab**: this is a venture capital firm, not a
  grant or sponsorship program. It does have a demonstrated, genuine
  interest in this exact space (portfolio includes Honest Jobs, Easy
  Expunctions, Justice Text). Any money from them would be equity or
  convertible-note investment, not sponsorship, with governance strings
  attached.
- **Persevere / Tennessee Technology Workforce Alliance**: looked like a
  corporate-sponsorship precedent but was actually a $15.4M federal EDA
  grant (American Rescue Plan funds). Named corporate partners (Epic
  Games, Banyan Labs, ProTech) contributed hiring pipelines and training
  design, not cash.

## What Was Not Tested (Open Leads, Worth a Look Later)

- **Smaller or regional fair-chance employers** outside the mega-cap
  coalition roster, with discretionary local marketing or CSR line items
  rather than centralized nonprofit-only foundation programs. This category
  was not tested and could behave very differently from national banks and
  Fortune 500 coalitions.
- **Individual outreach to Second Chance Business Coalition member
  companies**, one at a time, rather than through the coalition itself.
  A single regional manager with discretionary budget may be reachable even
  where the corporate program is not.
- **Stand Together Ventures Lab as an equity path**, if Troy is open to
  investment rather than sponsorship for a defined slice of the business.

## What This Means For The Near-Term Plan

The centralized, apply-here sponsor pipeline does not currently exist for
direct-to-founder cash on a 30-60 day timeline. That makes the
relationship-based path (an NBA/NBA Foundation conversation through Cori, a
nonprofit partner's board or CSR contacts, direct asks to individuals inside
fair-chance employers) the highest-probability lever right now, not further
cold research into named corporate programs. Warm intros beat cold
applications in this category as of this pass.
