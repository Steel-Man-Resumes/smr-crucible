## Executive Summary

Forge and Refinery should be funded differently from Midnight Greenhouse OS.

Midnight Greenhouse OS is a grant-growth SaaS/network product that can sell seats,
cohorts, sponsored circles, audits, and pursuit services. Forge and Refinery have
a different constraint: Troy has promised the product will be free, and it is
expected to be deployed nationally in August. That promise is valuable. It should
not be walked back.

The funding thesis:

> Forge and Refinery stay free for justice-impacted people because institutions,
> sponsors, funders, employers, and nonprofit partners pay for the ecosystem,
> implementation, support, reporting, and outcomes around the free tool.

Best immediate funding paths:

1. Anchor sponsor for the August national launch.
2. Nonprofit partner grant where the nonprofit applies and pays Steel Man Resumes
   / Troy as the implementation and technology partner.
3. Sponsored cohorts for re-entry organizations, workforce programs, mentors, or
   job seekers.
4. Paid institutional layer while the user-facing Forge remains free.
5. NBA Foundation / Cori-adjacent workforce youth path if a qualifying nonprofit
   partner is involved.
6. Claude Corps host/fellow strategy for nonprofit AI capacity support.
7. Tech nonprofit/fiscal sponsor strategy if Troy wants Forge/Refinery to attract
   philanthropic tech funding directly.

Fastest realistic money is not a traditional grant directly to Troy. The fastest
money is a sponsor or partner contract:

> Fund the August national launch of a free re-entry AI career platform, with
> measurable usage, workforce readiness, resumes generated, applications supported,
> partner onboarding, and fair-chance employer matching.

## Product Context

From the repo README:

- Steel Man Crucible is a career-services platform for justice-impacted people
  and the organizations that support them.
- It turns a person's story into a job: guided self-understanding, tailored resume,
  disclosure planning, interview practice, fair-chance employers, and applications.
- Steel Man Resumes operates two major surfaces:
  - The Forge: free, no login.
  - The Refinery: full authenticated journey.
- The product posture is coaching, not legal advice.
- The platform includes a consent-gated partner dashboard so support organizations
  can see only progress the user chooses to share.
- Privacy posture: store progress, not transcripts.

This matters for funding because the product can be framed as:

- re-entry workforce infrastructure,
- fair-chance hiring enablement,
- AI for social good,
- nonprofit capacity building,
- career navigation for justice-impacted people,
- employer talent pipeline infrastructure,
- public-benefit technology with institutional support.

## The Core Constraint: Free Must Be Preserved

The user has promised Forge and Refinery will be free. The national August launch
should reinforce that promise rather than create uncertainty.

Recommended rule:

> The person rebuilding their life never pays for core access.

That does not mean the business cannot make money. It means the payer should be:

- sponsors,
- foundations,
- employers,
- workforce boards,
- nonprofits,
- correctional/re-entry programs,
- universities,
- public agencies,
- corporate social responsibility programs,
- philanthropic AI programs,
- paid institutional users,
- data/reporting customers where privacy and consent permit.

## Legal, Ethical, And Structural Notes

### Avoid Percentage Of Grant Award

Do not take compensation based on a percentage of funds raised. The Association of
Fundraising Professionals Code of Ethical Standards says compensation may include
bonuses or merit pay but may never be based on a percentage of funds raised, and
members should decline finder fees/commissions based on percentage of funds raised.
Source: https://afpglobal.org/ethics/code-ethical-standards

Acceptable alternatives:

- fixed project fee,
- monthly retainer,
- platform subscription,
- implementation contract,
- hourly consulting,
- milestone bonus not calculated as a percentage of grant award,
- referral commission on product/service revenue,
- sponsor sales commission on sponsorship revenue.

### Be Careful With User Data

Funding reports should use aggregate and anonymized data unless explicit consent
exists. Partner dashboards must remain consent-gated.

Avoid selling individual user data. If data products are considered later, they
must be aggregate, de-identified, consent-aware, and aligned with the mission.

## Risks And Mitigations

Risk: Grant timelines are too slow for August.

Mitigation: pursue sponsor/contract money now; grants become next-wave funding.

Risk: Free promise creates unsustainable AI costs.

Mitigation: rate limits, sponsor-backed pools, institutional underwriting,
mock/low-cost modes, and paid institutional layer.

Risk: Funders are skeptical of AI in vulnerable populations.

Mitigation: emphasize coaching not legal advice, consent gates, no transcript
storage, human support, transparent methodology, privacy posture, and auditability.

Risk: For-profit entity blocks grants.

Mitigation: partner-led grants, fiscal sponsor, nonprofit arm later, and
contractor/vendor budget line.

Risk: Relationship-based paths are vague.

Mitigation: give every connector a concrete ask: sponsor, host, pilot org,
intro, cohort, or partner grant.

Risk: Sponsor wants branding that undermines trust.

Mitigation: sponsor recognition can exist on landing pages/reports, but not in
ways that make vulnerable users feel surveilled, sold, or targeted.
