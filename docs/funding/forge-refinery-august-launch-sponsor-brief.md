# Forge and Refinery
## Free AI Career Infrastructure for Justice-Impacted People
### August 2026 National Launch -- Sponsorship Brief

---

## The Problem

Coming home after incarceration means rebuilding a career from a story most
employers were never taught how to read. Career support for justice-impacted
people is fragmented: a resume workshop here, a disclosure conversation there,
a job board with no context for what a person actually survived. Organizations
that want to help often cannot afford the tools or staff time to do it well.

## The Solution

Steel Man Resumes built the Forge and the Refinery, a two-part platform that
turns a person's story into a job:

- **The Forge** -- free, no login required. Guided self-understanding, a
  first resume, and a clear next step.
- **The Refinery** -- the full guided journey. Tailored resumes, disclosure
  planning, interview practice, fair-chance employer connections, and an AI
  coach the user names and directs.
- **Partner Dashboard** -- consent-gated visibility so case managers,
  mentors, and re-entry organizations see only the progress a user chooses
  to share. Nothing is visible without the user's permission.

The posture throughout is coaching, not legal advice. The platform stores a
person's progress, never their transcripts or private words.

## The Promise

We have made one commitment that will not change: **the person rebuilding
their life never pays for core access.** Forge and Refinery launch nationally
in August 2026, free, for every justice-impacted person and re-entry
organization that needs them.

That promise is the point. It is also the reason we are raising launch
funding now, from partners who believe the same thing we do: that the cost
of rebuilding a life after incarceration should not fall on the person doing
the rebuilding.

## What Sponsorship Funds

A launch sponsor covers the infrastructure behind free access for the first
90 to 180 days of national deployment:

- hosting and AI usage at national scale
- participant onboarding and support
- national launch communications
- partner onboarding for re-entry organizations
- accessibility and plain-language review
- outcome tracking and impact reporting

## What a Sponsor Receives

- recognition as the August 2026 national launch sponsor
- a periodic impact report built from aggregate, anonymized outcomes
- visibility with the re-entry and fair-chance employment ecosystem
- alignment with a concrete, measurable, values-forward launch
- the option to sponsor a named cohort, city, or region

## Why Now

Forge and Refinery deploy nationally in August 2026. Every organization
serving justice-impacted job seekers, and every employer building a
fair-chance pipeline, needs a tool this specific to exist and stay free. A
launch sponsor makes that possible on day one, not eventually.
