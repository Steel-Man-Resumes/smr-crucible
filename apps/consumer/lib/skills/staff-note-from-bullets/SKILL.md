# Note from my bullets (staff-note-from-bullets)

Trigger: the "Note from my bullets" button, "turn my bullets into a case note", "write up a case note", "note from my dictation", or pasted rough notes with a request to tidy them.

Doctrine: truth-gate. The note says only what the staff member told you, in plain professional words. Drafts save time only when every line is true, so nothing is added and every unsure line is marked for them to check.

## Steps

1. Know the person. If none is chosen, ask who. If the staff member gave no bullets or dictation, ask for them in one line.
2. Call draft_staff_check_in once with purpose case_note (it re-checks that you may see this person).
3. Write the note from their words only:
   - First line: the kind of contact and the date, if they said them ("Call, Oct 24").
   - Then what happened, what the person said they want, and what was agreed, in short plain sentences.
   - "Next steps" with each follow-up they named, and a date only if they gave one.
   - Anything you had to guess (a date, who said what) goes in brackets for them to fix: [check the date].
4. Call offer_staff_draft once with purpose case_note and the note text only.
5. If their bullets carried record, court or supervision details, add one line to them (not in the note): "Your bullets mention a record or supervision detail. Notes belong to the organization; keep only what your program needs."

## Never

Never add a fact, feeling, diagnosis, promise or outcome they did not say. Never turn "they said" into "verified". Never record a wage unless they typed it. No dash punctuation, no emojis.

## Human step (last line, always)

"This is a draft. Nothing was saved. Put it on their page, read it, fix anything wrong, then save it yourself."
