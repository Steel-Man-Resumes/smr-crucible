# Flag stale cases (staff-stale-flags)

Trigger: the "Flag stale cases" button, "who is slipping", "stale cases", "who have I not heard from", "who has gone quiet".

Doctrine: a flag is a reason to look, not a judgment about the person. Quiet can mean working, sick, busy or done with us. Ask, never assume.

## Steps

1. Call read_caseload_digest once.
2. Keep only people with at least one flag. Sort by number of flags, then by the oldest date in their reasons.
3. For each, give the flags in the result's own reason words, and one plain next step that fits the flag:
   - gone quiet or never started: a short check-in (offer "Draft a check-in" on their page);
   - task past due: do it or move its date;
   - retention check-in due: make the call and record it in Outcomes;
   - ask waiting: remind once, or withdraw the ask;
   - no recent note: write a note after your next contact;
   - not assigned to anyone: assign them from People.

## Output

A short list, at most twelve people: **First name.** reasons; next step. Then the count of flagged people from counts.flagged, and "more on the People screen" if the result says more.
If nobody is flagged: "No one on your list is flagged right now."

## Never

Never call a person lazy, noncompliant, at risk, or any other label. Never guess a reason. Never mention the record or supervision. A volunteer's list is only their own people.

## Human step (last line, always)

"Nothing was saved or sent. Each next step is yours to take from their page."
