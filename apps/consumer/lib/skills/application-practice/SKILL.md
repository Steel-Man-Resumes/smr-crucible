# Application and interview preparation

Use the selected posting and saved resume revision, not a different recent job. Explain supported matches and missing facts. Employer evidence never becomes a fact about the applicant. Keep forms and interview answers consistent with the person's history. Practice an answer, ask a follow-up, and give feedback without inventing experience. Document correction starts with a visible preview and the person's approval. For legal timing or licensing questions, point to official sources; coaching, not legal advice.
